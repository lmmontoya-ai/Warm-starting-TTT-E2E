## Requirements / Constraints Relevant To Drafting

- OJ-CS is an open-access IEEE Computer Society journal covering emerging topics
  and trends across computing. The revised warm-start paper should therefore be
  written as a full characterization article, not a short letter.
- The OJ-CS call asks authors to format submissions with the IEEE Open Journals
  article template from the IEEE Author Center; Word and LaTeX templates are
  available there.
- IEEE Computer Society author guidance says journal submissions require an
  article template.
- IEEE Author Center guidance emphasizes selecting the right journal, checking
  aims and scope, following the target journal's submission guidelines, and
  submitting to only one publication at a time.
- Figures will need to be prepared as individual files according to IEEE figure
  requirements when the Markdown draft is moved into the IEEE template.

## Drafting Implications

- The main paper should foreground the characterization result and
  reproducibility rather than a short novelty claim.
- Detailed cloud-debugging cost should not appear in the manuscript; only the
  scientific cost-accounting caveat belongs in the paper, likely in an appendix.
- The bridge-budget frontier should become a main figure before conversion into
  the IEEE template.
